Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have
questions?

Does this website replace a tax advisor or lawyer?
+
No. DocuSwiss provides general information and document templates for guidance only. This site does not constitute personalized legal or tax advice. For complex or contentious situations, we recommend consulting a qualified professional (fiduciary, lawyer, notary).
Are the generated documents legally valid?
+
Our templates are based on statutory laws in force in Switzerland. However, their validity depends on the accuracy of the information provided and your specific case. Always review the contents before submitting any document to an authority.
Can I submit my tax return directly through this website?
+
Currently, the site helps you prepare and complete your return. Submission is done via your canton's official portal (e.g., VaudTax, GeTax, ZHprivateTax). We guide you to the right platform and provide instructions.
Is the service free?
+
Yes, access to information, legal articles, and basic templates is completely free. Advanced automated completion tools may be offered in a premium version in the future.
In which cantons is the website available?
+
DocuSwiss currently covers the cantons of Vaud, Geneva, Zurich, and Bern for tax returns. Legal document templates (appeals, complaints, powers of attorney) are valid throughout Switzerland. More cantons will be added gradually.
